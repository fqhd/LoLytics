## LoLytics — League of Legends Analytics Platform
[![CI](https://github.com/fqhd/LoLytics/actions/workflows/ci.yml/badge.svg)](https://github.com/fqhd/LoLytics/actions/workflows/ci.yml)

**LoLytics** is an interactive analytics platform built for *League of Legends* players who want to improve by reviewing their matches.

Whether you're a casual player or a ranked grinder, Lolytics helps you break down games with win probability graphs, build optimization insights, and macro decision review tools.

### Features

- Search for your **Summoner name** and view your **match history**
- Click into a match to explore your **win probability over time**
- Interactively **edit the game state** and watch win probability update in real-time
- Review your **item build** and **rune setup** to check for optimal choices
